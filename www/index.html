slippypack

Cut a piece of the map and carry it on your watch. It renders here, in this tab — nothing is uploaded, and no server builds anything.

Area

Drag on the map to draw an area. Shift-drag works any time. The grid is the coarsest zoom's tiles: a pack holds whole tiles, so it reaches a little past the box you draw.

Detail

The watch's reader accepts an RLE8 pack, verifies it, reports a live map — and draws nothing, because it has no RLE decoder. Until it has one, keep this on None and keep the area small.
