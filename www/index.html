slippypack

Cut a piece of the map and carry it on your watch. It renders here, in this tab — nothing is uploaded, and no server builds anything.

Area

Drag on the map to draw an area; shift-drag works any time. The presets set a radius and the detail that fits it — 10 km costs about what 5 km does, by giving up one zoom level. The grid shows the coarsest zoom's tiles: a pack holds whole tiles, so it reaches a little past your box.

Detail

The spec says 128 px and RLE8. The watch agrees with neither: it skips a 128 px pack as though there were no map here, and an RLE8 pack passes every check, reports a live map and draws nothing. Both fail without an error, so the defaults above are what works on hardware — and uncompressed is 18× larger, which is why the area has to stay small.
