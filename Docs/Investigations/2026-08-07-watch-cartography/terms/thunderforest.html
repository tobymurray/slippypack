Payment Policy

Thunderforest offers a monthly subscription option. We don’t offer a pay annually option at the moment.

  • Payments are processed monthly, on the same day each month based on the date of your account upgrade.
  • Payments auto-renew each month unless you upgrade, downgrade or cancel your account.
  • We accept payments by credit or debit card. We currently accept Visa, MasterCard and American Express.
  • If your payment fails, we will send you a notification and give you a grace period to contact your bank or update your card information.
  • We may from time to time change the prices for our plans. If we make changes that affect your plan, we will give you at least 30 days notice.
  • Customers may cancel at any time for any reason. Cancellation does not entitle customers to a refund of any fees already paid.

Bulk Downloading

Absolutely no bulk-downloading scraping, pre-downloading, pre-caching or anything similar without an appropriate plan.

  • If you wish to make bulk downloads, please upgrade your account to the Small Business plan or higher.
  • We reserve the right to block or restrict your account if you bulk download on an inappropriate plan.
  • Please get in touch if you’d like to discuss bulk downloading. We’re very friendly if you approach us for a chat. We just get annoyed when people try and take advantage.

High Traffic Services

Some sites and applications prove to be extremely popular, sometimes unexpectedly. If this turns out to be the case for you, then we will get in touch and ask you to upgrade to an appropriate service plan.

  • We won’t charge you for a short-term traffic surge - instead, we can temporarily throttle traffic to avoid you going over your quota.
  • If you’d rather pay for all surge traffic and ensure a constant service during unexpected events, then please get in touch.
